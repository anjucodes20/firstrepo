Cardiovascular Chest / Mediastinum

Heart

Cor

The heart is a muscular, double-sided biological pump that beats approx. 100,000 times each day, circulating 7,200 liters of blood to sustain cellular life.

  • Generates pressure to circulate blood through 60,000 miles of vessels
  • Sinoatrial (SA) node acts as natural autonomous pacemaker
  • Separates low-pressure pulmonary and high-pressure systemic circuits
